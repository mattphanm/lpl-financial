# Client Communication Templates

Templates for notifying clients at each transfer stage. Placeholders in `{{braces}}`.

## Missing Information
> Hi {{clientName}}, we've started processing your transfer from {{deliveringFirm}}.
> To continue, we need: {{missingItems}}. Please upload these at your earliest convenience.

## In Review
> Your transfer (ref {{transferId}}) is under review. No action is needed from you right now.

## Escalated
> Your transfer (ref {{transferId}}) requires additional review by our operations team.
> We'll follow up within 1–2 business days.

## Ready / Submitted
> Good news — your transfer from {{deliveringFirm}} has been submitted. Settlement typically
> completes within 5–7 business days.

## Completed
> Your transfer (ref {{transferId}}) is complete. All assets are now available in your
> {{receivingFirm}} account.
