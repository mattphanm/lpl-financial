# Transfer Status Definitions

**Document type:** Internal reference / compliance
**Audience:** Financial advisors

> Synthetic internal guidance for the TransferReady prototype. Not investment, tax, or legal advice.

These definitions standardize how transfer status is described to advisors and, where appropriate, to clients.

## Status definitions

- **NOT_STARTED** — The transfer request exists but no work has begun. No documents have been requested yet.

- **IN_PROGRESS** — The transfer is actively being worked. Documents are being collected and the case is moving through the early stages.

- **WAITING_ON_CLIENT** — The firm has completed its current step and is waiting on the client to provide a document, a signature, or an answer. The correct next action is **client outreach**. This is a client-action blocker.

- **INTERNAL_REVIEW** — The transfer is in the firm's internal review queue (for example, compliance or operations). The correct next action is **internal follow-up**, not client outreach. The client generally does not need to do anything at this stage.

- **CUSTODIAN_PROCESSING** — Documentation is complete and the receiving custodian is coordinating the transfer with the delivering institution. Timing is outside advisor control; do not promise a completion date.

- **COMPLETE** — Assets have settled at the receiving custodian and the transfer is finished. A complete transfer is low risk by definition.

## Using status to choose the next action

- WAITING_ON_CLIENT → remind the client of the specific outstanding item.
- INTERNAL_REVIEW → follow up internally; do not contact the client about a blocker they cannot resolve.
- CUSTODIAN_PROCESSING → monitor; communicate status without guaranteeing timing.
- COMPLETE → no action required beyond confirmation.
