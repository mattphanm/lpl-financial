# Signature Requirements

**Document type:** Internal requirements guide
**Audience:** Financial advisors

> Synthetic internal guidance for the TransferReady prototype. Not investment, tax, or legal advice.

## When signatures are required

An account holder signature is required on the transfer request form for all account transfers. For jointly held accounts, all account holders must sign. Some delivering institutions additionally require a signature guarantee (medallion stamp) for transfers above a threshold.

## What happens when a signature is missing

A missing required signature blocks the transfer at the Documentation stage. The transfer cannot proceed to internal review or custodian processing until the signed form is received.

A signature with status MISSING should be treated as a client-action blocker: the next step is client outreach to obtain the signature.

## Escalation rules

- If the signature remains outstanding for **5 or more days** after the request, send a follow-up reminder to the client.
- If the signature remains outstanding for **10 or more days**, escalate to a transition specialist for review and consider an alternate contact method.
- If the delivering institution requires a medallion signature guarantee and the client cannot obtain one, escalate internally rather than instructing the client on how to proceed.
