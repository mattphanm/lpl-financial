# Identity Verification Procedure

**Document type:** Internal procedure
**Audience:** Financial advisors

> Synthetic internal guidance for the TransferReady prototype. Not investment, tax, or legal advice.

## Required verification

Identity verification is required for every account transfer before assets can move. Standard verification confirms the account holder's legal name, date of birth, and a government-issued identifier on file. The verification status progresses: MISSING → REQUESTED → RECEIVED → VERIFIED.

## Incomplete verification workflow

- If identity verification is MISSING or REQUESTED, the transfer is blocked from advancing to custodian processing.
- When documents are received but not yet confirmed (RECEIVED), the transfer remains in Documentation until an internal reviewer marks it VERIFIED.
- Mismatched information (for example, a name that does not match the delivering institution's records) must be resolved before the transfer continues.

## Escalation path

- If verification documents are RECEIVED but cannot be confirmed within **3 business days**, escalate to internal review.
- If there is a data mismatch that the client cannot immediately resolve, escalate to a transition specialist rather than advising the client on how to resolve a legal-name discrepancy.
- Do not proceed with the transfer on the basis of unverified identity.
