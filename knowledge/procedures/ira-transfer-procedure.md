# IRA Transfer Procedure

**Document type:** Internal procedure
**Applies to:** Traditional IRA and Roth IRA account transfers
**Audience:** Financial advisors managing client account transfers

> This is synthetic internal guidance created for the TransferReady prototype. It does not represent any real firm's policy and is not investment, tax, or legal advice.

## Overview

An IRA transfer moves retirement assets from an external institution to the receiving custodian. The transfer proceeds through defined stages and cannot advance until the required documentation for each stage is complete.

## Transfer stages

1. **Initiation** — The transfer request is opened and the client is sent the initial document checklist.
2. **Documentation** — Required forms are collected: transfer request form, beneficiary designation form, and identity verification. The transfer cannot move to review until all required documents are received.
3. **Review** — Internal review confirms the paperwork is complete and consistent. Items flagged here may require client follow-up.
4. **Custodian** — The receiving custodian processes the transfer with the delivering institution. Timing depends on the delivering institution and is outside advisor control.
5. **Completion** — Assets settle at the receiving custodian and the transfer is marked complete.

## Documents required

- **Transfer request form** (required): authorizes the movement of assets.
- **Beneficiary designation form** (required): must be completed before the transfer can proceed to custodian review.
- **Identity verification** (required): confirms the account holder's identity.
- **Account statement** (as needed): a recent statement from the delivering institution may be requested to confirm holdings.

## Expected workflow

- Confirm all required documentation is received during the Documentation stage.
- A required document with a status of MISSING blocks progression to the next stage.
- Once documentation is complete, submit for internal review.
- After review, the transfer enters custodian processing.

## Custodian review

Custodian review begins only after all required documentation is complete and internal review has signed off. Custodian processing time varies by delivering institution. Advisors should not promise a specific completion date, because custodian timing is outside the firm's control.
