# Brokerage Transfer Procedure

**Document type:** Internal procedure
**Applies to:** Taxable brokerage account transfers
**Audience:** Financial advisors managing client account transfers

> Synthetic internal guidance for the TransferReady prototype. Not investment, tax, or legal advice.

## Overview

A brokerage transfer moves taxable investment assets from an external institution to the receiving custodian, typically via an ACATS-style process. The transfer advances through stages and requires complete documentation at each step.

## Transfer stages

1. **Initiation** — Transfer request opened; client receives the document checklist.
2. **Documentation** — Collect the transfer request form, account holder signature, and identity verification. A recent account statement may be requested to confirm positions.
3. **Review** — Internal review validates the paperwork and the list of transferring positions.
4. **Custodian** — The receiving custodian coordinates the position transfer with the delivering firm.
5. **Completion** — Positions settle and the transfer is marked complete.

## Documents required

- **Transfer request form** (required).
- **Account holder signature** (required): a missing signature blocks progression.
- **Identity verification** (required).
- **Account statement** (as needed): confirms the positions and cost basis to transfer.

## Expected workflow

- Verify the transferring positions are eligible for in-kind transfer; non-transferable positions may need to be liquidated first, which requires explicit client instruction.
- A required document with status MISSING blocks the next stage.
- Submit to internal review once documentation is complete.

## Custodian review

The receiving custodian handles the mechanical transfer with the delivering institution. Processing time depends on the delivering firm and the position types. Do not guarantee a settlement date to the client.
