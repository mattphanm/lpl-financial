# Client Communication Guidelines

**Document type:** Internal communication policy
**Audience:** Financial advisors

> Synthetic internal guidance for the TransferReady prototype. Not investment, tax, or legal advice.

## Communication tone

- Be clear, concise, and professional.
- Lead with the specific action the client needs to take.
- Respect the client's stated contact preference (email or phone) and communication style.
- Avoid jargon; explain any required form in plain language.

## What can be stated

- The current status and stage of the transfer.
- Which specific document or action is outstanding.
- That a required document must be completed before the transfer can proceed.
- General, factual descriptions of the transfer process.

## What cannot be promised

- A specific completion or settlement date. Custodian and delivering-institution timing is outside the firm's control.
- Any investment outcome, performance, or tax result.
- That an action has occurred unless it is confirmed in the record.

## Advisor review requirement

**All client communication generated with assistance must be reviewed and approved by the advisor before it is sent.** Generated drafts are starting points, not final messages. The advisor is responsible for accuracy, tone, and compliance of anything sent to a client.

Do not provide investment advice, tax advice, or legal advice in client communications. If a client question calls for such advice, direct them to the appropriate professional.
